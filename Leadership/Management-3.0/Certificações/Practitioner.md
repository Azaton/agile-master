Projeto

You'll have to publish **seven blog posts, videos or podcasts** about how you applied Management 3.0 practices telling us what you learned and gained and experienced. Each of these articles and pieces of content will be checked and verified to make sure that they're up to Management 3.0 standards and that you've truly grasped the essence of each practice and how it works.
