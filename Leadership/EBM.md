The PAL-EBM (Professional Agile Leadership - Evidence-Based Management) certification offered by [Scrum.org](https://www.scrum.org/assessments/professional-agile-leadership-evidence-based-management-certification) is designed for leaders and managers who want to understand and apply evidence-based practices to make informed decisions and drive organizational agility.

The PAL-EBM certification focuses on helping leaders understand how to measure and improve outcomes in complex, dynamic environments. It provides them with the knowledge and tools to establish a culture of empiricism and data-driven decision-making within their organizations.

The certification program covers several key areas:

1. Evidence-Based Management Foundations: Participants learn the principles and practices of evidence-based management, including the importance of transparency, inspection, and adaptation. They understand how to create an environment that supports empirical decision-making.

2. Measure: This module focuses on identifying meaningful outcomes and defining appropriate key performance indicators (KPIs) that align with organizational goals. Participants learn how to measure progress and make data-driven decisions.

3. Measure Value: Participants explore different techniques to measure the value delivered by products and initiatives. They learn how to evaluate the impact of changes and investments to maximize value for customers and the organization.

4. Experiment & Learn: This module emphasizes the importance of experimentation and continuous learning. Participants discover how to frame hypotheses, design experiments, and gather feedback to validate assumptions and make informed decisions.

5. Organizational Agility: Participants gain insights into creating an agile organization that embraces change and adapts to evolving market conditions. They learn how to foster collaboration, empower teams, and enable continuous improvement.

The PAL-EBM certification involves studying the provided learning materials, attending a two-day training course, and passing an online assessment. The certification demonstrates that the individual has a deep understanding of evidence-based management practices and is equipped to lead and guide organizations towards improved outcomes and agility.
